Convergence Lab

Empirical proof: the stochastic render converges to a deterministic target with error ~ 1/√N

Error RMS vs N (log–log)

The target is the exact integral ∫ p(τ)·source(τ) dτ — a deterministic, blurred grain. Each ray is a Monte Carlo estimator of that integral. If the theory is real, the error falls as 1/√N and variance-reduction (stratified / importance) sits below random.
